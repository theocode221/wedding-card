Laila / Hazziq gallery.

Put the client's 12 prewedding photos in this folder as JPEG, named in display order:
  1.jpg  2.jpg  3.jpg ... 12.jpg

They show on /laila/galeri automatically, sorted by filename, in 4 chapters of 3.
Photos are shown uncropped at their original shape (portrait or landscape both fine).

Once any .jpg/.jpeg is in this folder, only JPEGs are used (the old PNG placeholders
are ignored). At most 12 photos are shown.

Recommended export: about 1600-2000 px on the long edge, JPEG quality ~80-85
(roughly 300-700 KB each) so the page stays sharp but loads fast on phones.

Note: 8.png, 9.png, and 10.png are design mockups (wood frame + placeholder text),
not photos.
